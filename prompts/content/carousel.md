# Format: Instagram / LinkedIn carousel (6–10 slides)

- Slide 1: the hook (a question or a surprising true line), 8 words or fewer.
- Slides 2–N-2: one idea per slide — source says / history shows / what it means / the honest counterpoint.
  Headline 8 words or fewer; body 35 words or fewer; `visual_spec` says the chart, document crop or icon.
- Slide N-1: the verdict (Works / Doesn't / Works only when) or "what to watch".
- Slide N: the usable takeaway + "full research: link in bio".
Language: Hinglish in Roman script for Instagram readability, key terms in English. `caption`: 3–6 lines, sources
at the end. `hashtags`: 5–10.
