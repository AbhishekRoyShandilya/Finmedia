# Format: Instagram Reel / YouTube Short (35–60 seconds)

Scene-by-scene plan, 80–140 spoken words:
1. Hook (0–2 s): a surprising, true line from the research. `hook_text` is the on-screen version.
2. Reframe: why the obvious reading is incomplete.
3. Mechanism: one clear visual explanation.
4. Payoff: what it means for the viewer, or what to watch (include proposal vs final status when relevant).
5. Close: one calm line pointing to the full research.

Each scene has one `visual`: AVATAR, CHART, DOC_RECEIPT, ANIMATION, TEXT_CARD or BROLL. At least two non-AVATAR
visual types; AVATAR in at most half the scenes. Put `[beat]` before each key number or reveal. At most one spoken
number per sentence; exact figures go in `on_screen_text`. `title_variants`: exactly three (Hinglish, Hindi,
English). `caption`: 2–4 short Hinglish lines ending with the sources line. `hashtags`: 5–8. `series`: "Research".
