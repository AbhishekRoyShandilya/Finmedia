# Format: newsletter / WhatsApp long note (600–1,000 words)

Written in clear English (a Hindi version can be generated separately), for readers who want the reasoning:
- `subject` (under 60 characters) and `preheader` (under 90).
- `intro`: the question and the answer in three sentences.
- `sections` (3–5): what the primary sources say; what history shows (with the sample sizes the object gives);
  what we think and why; the strongest counter-argument; what to watch and when.
- `takeaway`: the usable rule, checklist or test.
Short paragraphs. Every number exactly as in the object.
