# Format: YouTube research video (8–15 minutes)

Structure:
1. **Hook (first 15 s):** the most useful true thing in the research, as a question or a reversal. No channel intro.
2. **Chapters (5–8):** each one idea, in order: the question → what the primary sources say → what history shows →
   what it means → the honest counterpoint (the red team's strongest challenge) → what to watch.
3. **Verdict card:** Works / Doesn't work / Works only when — with the conditions from the object.
4. **Takeaway:** a checklist or test the viewer can use today.

Visual effort (every chapter lists visuals): at least three different non-avatar visual types across the video
(CHART, DOC_RECEIPT showing the exact section of the filing or circular, ANIMATION, TEXT_CARD, BROLL). The founder's
disclosed avatar opens and closes and appears at most in a third of chapters.

`title_variants`: exactly three (Hinglish in Roman script, Hindi in Devanagari, English); curious and specific,
never ALL CAPS. `thumbnail_text`: 2–5 words. `description`: 3–5 lines + sources + disclosure.
